How it's calculated

The target density for bush beans is 18.76 plants per square meter. On a square grid, that means one plant every 39.37 ÷ √18.76 ≈ 9.1 inches (39.37 inches in a meter).

Plants per row = bed length ÷ spacing, rounded down. Rows = bed width ÷ spacing, rounded down. Total = plants per row × rows. The grid is centered in the bed, so any leftover space is split evenly around the edges.
